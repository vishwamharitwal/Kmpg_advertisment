# K.M. Advertising & Publicity Services - Review System

A streamlined review and smart feedback redirection system for **K.M. Advertising & Publicity Services**.

## Features
- **Smart Feedback Redirection & Soft Gating**:
  - **3, 4 & 5 Stars**: Review text is copied to clipboard and redirected to Google Review page (`https://g.page/r/CfBwzW-LSEE0EBM/review`).
  - **1 & 2 Stars**: Direct WhatsApp resolution (`+91 87400 64577`) with a policy-compliant secondary link to continue on Google.
- **Dynamic Review Synthesis**: Generates unique, natural, short reviews (8-14 words) on the fly to bypass duplicate spam filters.
- **Brand Aesthetic**: Warm Kraft paper palette with high contrast text.
